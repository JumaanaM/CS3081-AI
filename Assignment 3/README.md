CS3081 Artificial Intelligence - Assignment 3: Uncertainty & Bayesian Networks 

Overview
- This assignment covers probability theory, Naive Bayes classification, exact inference via enumeration, and approximate inference (Rejection Sampling and Likelihood Weighting) using a Bayesian Network modeling Heatwave (H), AC Overload (A), Power Cut (C), and Lab Cancellation (L).

Summary of Tasks
- Part 1 (Probability & Independence): Verified probability axioms, computed conditional probabilities and proved dependence.
- Part 2 (Naive Bayes): Built spam filter classifier using Bayes' rule, updating P(Spam) given the words free and winner.
- Part 3 (Bayesian Networks): Factored & calculated joint/conditional probabilities, and demonstrated conditional independence.
- Part 4 (Exact Inference): Used enumeration to compute conditional probabilities.
- Part 5 (Approximate Inference): Estimated P(H | L) using Rejection Sampling and Likelihood Weighting.
